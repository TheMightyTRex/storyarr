# Storyarr v0.4.8

## Changed

- **Upgrade available.** When a newer version is out, the version number in the menu has a red dot and "Upgrade available" beside it. Select either to read the release notes.
- **Quieter upgrades.** The upgrade wizard opens only when a new version brings settings to choose, or changes to your configuration or database. Other upgrades finish on their own: Storyarr still saves a settings backup first, and the release notes are under the version number.
- **Restore previous version.** The way back has moved from the wizard to Settings, System, Upgrades and backups. It takes you through restoring your settings from the backup taken before the upgrade, undoing any database changes, and starting the previous version again.
- **One typeface.** Every page now uses Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a typeface designed for readers with low vision. The Low-vision font switch in Display and accessibility is gone, because it is always on.
- **Slide-out panels.** The My library summary, the bookshelf drawer and the calendar day share one header: the panel's name in bold on a white bar with a grey line, and the close button.

- **One toolbar on the Library tabs.** In my libraries, Authors, Series and All saved titles share one section header and toolbar: the heading and count, the view switch, search, Audiobooks and Ebooks chips, the Library filter and Sort. Filters you apply show as chips you can remove one by one, or all at once with Clear all.
- **Save as default.** Each Library tab can save its filters and order as your default. It is saved with your account, so it follows you to every browser. Settings, General, Saved views lists them and has Reset to default.
- **Phone menu.** On phones, the bottom bar is replaced by a menu button in the header. It opens a drawer with Library, Discover, Activity and Settings, each folding open to its pages, plus your account, Sign out and the version.

## Database

No database changes. To go back to v0.4.7, change the image tag and recreate the container.
