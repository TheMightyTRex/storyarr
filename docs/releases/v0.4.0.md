# Storyarr v0.4.0

The first Storyarr release. Storyarr is a fork of Dewarr v0.3.9 by logabell. It follows authors and series and
keeps your audiobook library complete, with a new look, bookshelf pages and a much faster database.

Coming from Dewarr? Read [Moving from Dewarr](https://github.com/TheMightyTRex/storyarr/blob/main/docs/MIGRATING.md) before you switch images. Your database and settings
carry over, and the upgrade wizard saves a settings backup first.

## Following and your library

- A bookshelf page for each followed author or series: a cover wall with a status on every book, a progress bar,
  status filters and a side drawer. The drawer can start a search, or exclude a book from the follow.
- One status for every followed book: In library, Downloading, Importing, Searching, Waiting (with the next search
  time), Not released, Needs attention, Wanted, Excluded (with the reason) and Not requested.
- Book types for follows: leave out comics, plays, companions and non-fiction, with your own defaults.
- **Add missing books** on series and authors, with the full list of books shown before it starts.
- Wanted books are searched again automatically: after 6 hours, then daily, then weekly.
- Delete a book from your library, synced with Audiobookshelf. Files go to a holding folder you can restore from.
- Audible search when matching library audiobooks, with a choice of Audible store.

## Look and accessibility

- Two layouts: Classic, or Shelf with a slim icon menu and section menus in the header.
- A light theme by default, plus Dark and Match device, meeting WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- Display and accessibility settings: text size up to 175%, a low-vision font, high contrast, reduced motion,
  underlined links and wider spacing.
- Activity in three tabs (Queue, Wanted and History), Settings in six groups, and a calendar Show menu.
- Each browser tab is named after its page. Plain British English throughout.

## Upgrades

- An upgrade wizard: before an existing install upgrades, Storyarr saves a settings backup. Afterwards it shows
  what is new, the new settings to review, any database changes and the way back.
- Automatic upgrades for people who prefer them: `BOOK_UPGRADE_AUTOMATIC=true`, or one setting.

## Speed

- Two new database indexes make request counts take under a second instead of minutes on large libraries.
- Leaving a page stops the database work it started.

## Database changes

Three migrations run on first start: `0081_automation_settings`, `0082_installation_automation` and
`0083_operation_lookups`. To go back to Dewarr v0.3.9, downgrade first: see [Moving from Dewarr](https://github.com/TheMightyTRex/storyarr/blob/main/docs/MIGRATING.md).
