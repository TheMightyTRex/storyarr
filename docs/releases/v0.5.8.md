# Storyarr v0.5.8

## Added

- **Series header with a logo.** A series page now opens with the series logo above its name, in one colour that suits your theme. Without a logo, the name is set large instead. Beside it are the author, the number of books, the next book, a short description, how many you have, **Add missing books** and **Follow**, with a fan of the first covers.
- **Change logo.** Admins can give a series a logo three ways: cut it out of one of the series' covers, find one on Wikimedia Commons with its licence shown, or upload a file. Logos are shared by everyone and saved on your server. Radio show pages have the same logo tools.
- **Actions on every cover.** Each cover has **Details**, **Ebook**, **Audiobook**, **Add to a list** and **More**. Pressing the cover opens the book's summary. A format already in your library shows a tick. Choose whether the buttons sit on the cover or beside it in Settings, General, **Cover actions**. Your choice is saved with your account.
- **Actions on rows and small covers.** A series' reading order, search results and Following have the same actions at the end of each row. Small covers, such as series gaps and calendar days, have one **More** button that names each action.
- **Radio, an early feature.** Add BBC radio shows by link or by name, then browse shows, stations, series and episodes in My library, Radio. Artwork and station logos are saved on your server. Storyarr does not download episodes or file them in Audiobookshelf yet: that comes in a later version.

## Changed

- **A new header.** Each page's tabs have a row of their own in the header, with icons. On phones, the menu holds search as well as every section and its pages.
- **One way for every slide-out.** The summary, the bookshelf drawer, the calendar day and the phone menu all open and close the same way, and every pop-up has the same header.
- **Teal accent** in every theme.
- **Status chips with icons.** Every page uses the same words for a book's status, each with an icon, so nothing relies on colour alone.
- **Easier to press.** Buttons, fields and menus are 44px high. Each Settings section has one bar to save or discard your changes, saying how many are unsaved.
- **Discover shelves.** No more scroll bars: the covers fade at each end, with an arrow when there are more books that way.
- **Covers line up.** Long titles no longer push covers out of line. Covers on In my libraries are 10% larger, and Settings, General has a cover size for My library: Small, Medium or Large.
- **Release notes.** The release notes show your current version and the one available, the steps to upgrade, and **Upgrade now** when `BOOK_UPGRADE_URL` points to your own upgrade page. Every release is listed in Settings, System, Upgrades and backups, Releases.
- **Lists menu.** On Discover, adding and refreshing lists is one **Lists** menu.
- **One layout.** The Classic layout is retired, and everyone uses Shelf. If you chose Classic, Storyarr opens in Shelf.

## Fixed

- **Unraid and mergerfs.** Imports into a library on an Unraid user share with a cache pool, or on a mergerfs pool, no longer stop with "Destination mount changed before publication". The fix has been in every Storyarr release; the download client guide now explains it.
- Release dates show in British style whatever your browser's language.

## Documentation

- **NZBGet** is now listed beside SABnzbd for Usenet in the README and the download client guide, with setup steps. It has been supported since v0.2.0.

## Database

New tables for radio shows and for series logos. Logo and radio image files are kept in your config folder.

To go back to v0.4.8, run `python -m app.downgrade storyarr_0086_notification_title` in the Storyarr container, then change the image tag and recreate the container. Only the radio tables, the shows in them and the series logos are removed; the image files stay in the config folder.
