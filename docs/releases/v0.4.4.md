# Storyarr v0.4.4

Sorting for your collection and follows, a way to choose releases Storyarr could not confirm, and series that group properly.

## Added

- **Sort by.** Series, Authors and Following can be sorted by Newest, Number of books, Date published, Author or A to Z. Reverse order turns any of them round. Author sorts by surname, so Ursula Le Guin is under L.
- **Download releases Storyarr could not confirm.** When a release's name does not clearly match the book, or the source does not say its language, an administrator can choose Review release, check the file list and confirm each point before downloading it. Each choice is recorded in the audit log, and the files are still checked after download.
- **Download unconfirmed releases** is a new permission in Users. Give it to people who are not administrators so they can do the same.
- **Review sources on Requests.** A request waiting for a source has Review sources, which shows the request's status and the book's sources in a dialog, so you can choose a release without leaving Requests.

## Changed

- **Co-written books match more releases.** Names that credit every author before or after the title, such as "Terry Pratchett & Stephen Baxter - The Long Earth", now count as the same book.
- **Whole-series releases are treated as collections**, for example "The Long Earth Series by..." or "books 1 to 5".
- **Series group together** when their names differ only in punctuation, a leading "The" or a trailing "Series". "Discworld: Industrial Revolution" and "Discworld - Industrial Revolution" are now one series.
- **My library starts with the books you have.** A followed series or author in My library opens on In library.
- Older Following links that sorted by Recently followed or Most books in library keep working.

## Database changes

None.
