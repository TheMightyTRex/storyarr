# Storyarr v0.5.9

## Changed

- **Display and accessibility is a slide-out.** The Display button opens a panel from the right, like every other, so you can see the page change as you choose. **Done** and **Reset** stay at the bottom, and **Match device** is the first theme.
- **Slide-out headers.** The top of each slide-out, and of the phone menu, now matches the page behind it, so the panel below reads as the part that slid out.
- **One look for the chosen option.** The current page in the main menu, the current onboarding step, the chosen part of a switch, the chosen Activity filter and the boxes in Settings are all teal, with a tick or a bar as well as the colour.
- **Buttons keep their size.** Buttons such as **Save changes** and **Change logo** are the same size and weight as the rest.
- **One focus ring.** Pressing Tab shows the same 3px ring on every control. In High contrast it uses the High contrast colour.
- **High contrast is stronger** on panels, muted text and dividers.
- **British dates, times and numbers on every page,** whatever language your browser uses: "2 November 2026" and "17:30".
- **Clearer wording.** "Catalogue" is spelt the British way, empty table cells say **Not known** or **None** instead of a dash, and a few headings and messages are plainer.

## Fixed

- **Deleting a notification channel asks first.** Notifications, **Delete** now opens a confirmation that says the delivery history goes too.
- **Users and access fits on phones.** At narrow widths and large text, each person is a block with their name, role and actions, with no sideways scrolling. Its buttons are the standard compact height.

## Your settings and the way back

Nothing to choose, and no database changes. To go back to v0.5.8, run that version again.
